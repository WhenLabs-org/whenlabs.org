About the studio.

EST. MMXXIV · ARLINGTON.TX

WhenLabs is a one-person studio run by me out of Arlington, TX. The work splits, roughly evenly, between open-source software (the workshop), short-form research about language models and attention (the preprints), and the slow accumulation of a small body of writing about how we actually work with computers.

Everything here is made with the aim of being useful first, beautiful second, and clever a distant third. If something looks finished, it usually is. If it doesn’t, it’s probably v0.
